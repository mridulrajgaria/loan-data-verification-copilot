The Problem 02 / 08

The Multi-Billion Dollar Mortgage Data Crisis

Secondary mortgage markets and capital market aggregators ingest loan portfolios from fragmented, inconsistent channels: spreadsheets, servicer feeds, and legacy APIs.

Underwriters struggle to detect data quality issues, leading to critical risk exposure and structural double-pledging.

Messy CSV Ingestion

Missing loan IDs, malformed ISO calendar dates, and unstandardized postal codes.

Servicing & Manifest Discrepancies

Conflicts in interest rates and balances between tapes, servicer records, and physical documents.

Rogue Database Alterations

Direct SQL injections or unlogged data manipulation that bypass audit controls.

Focus: Restoring data trust, governance, and traceability in credit portfolios.
The Solution 03 / 08

Ingestion & Linage

Streaming CSV parser storing raw verbatim payloads (`RawLoanRecord`) and file SHA-256 hashes (`RawUpload`) as strict non-nullable database relationships. Zero data is corrected silently during ingestion.

AI-Assisted Diagnostics

An isolated LLM assistant panel that interprets 15 underwriting rule exceptions, proposes exact JSON correction patches, drafts underwriter compliance notes, and translates natural language criteria into engine rules.

Cryptographic Seal

AGG-256 deterministic key-sorted JSON hashing (`canonicalStringify`) of signed records, locking them in `VerifiedLoan`. Proves record immutability and flags database-level tampering immediately.

Outcome: A single source of truth backed by mathematical proofs and human sign-off.

AI Controls & Governance 06 / 08

Separation of Concerns: AI Advice vs Human Decision

Per regulatory model risk guidance (e.g. OCC 2011-12), the platform enforces strict human-in-the-loop controls: AI recommends, but only humans execute.

Audit Trail Logging: Every AI request, prompt sent, model version, and generated suggestion is logged in `AIRecommendation` and is permanently indexed in the audit trail.

Isolated AIRecommendation Layer

AI recommendation starts with `acceptedByReviewer: null`. AI cannot mutate loan attributes or exception status directly.

Immutable ReviewAction Records

When an underwriter accepts, edits, or rejects an AI fix, before/after JSON snapshots are vaulted in `ReviewAction` linking back to the advisor record.

AI Control Shielding: Prevents "side-door" database manipulation and ensures audit integrity.
Cryptographic Attestation 07 / 08

Deterministic Tamper-Evidence

Audit logs are rows inside a database that could theoretically be modified or dropped by an administrator with direct database write access.

VerifiedLoan recordHash provides mathematical proof of integrity independent of the database audit log.

1. Canonical Key Sorting

Recursive alphabetical sorting ensures identical byte-for-byte serialization regardless of runtime JSON keys ordering.

2. SHA-256 Attestation Hashing

Aggregates loan fields, raw lineage source references, validation snapshot, and reviewer signature into a unique 64-character hash.

3. Live Tamper Verification

Alterations of even 1 byte in the database immediately triggers a `HASH_MISMATCH_TAMPER_DETECTED` warning on the auditor dashboard.

Demo Action: Open the Auditor Workspace and click "Demo Tamper" to show judges live detection.
